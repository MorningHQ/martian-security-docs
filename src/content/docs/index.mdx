## Start with your workspace

Want a local preview first? [Try a free two-record SDK evaluation](/sdk/evaluations/) without a Martian account or model key.

New to Martian? [Accept your invitation and sign in](/get-started/access/), then read [the concepts](/get-started/concepts/). You need an agent connection that you are authorized to test. Each tutorial explains the role, setup and limits before you start.
